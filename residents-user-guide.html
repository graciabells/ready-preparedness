Residents User Guide

Welcome to Ready Preparedness! This guide will help you understand how to use the website and access important disaster preparedness information.

How to Use Ready Preparedness

Follow these simple instructions to explore the website.

1. Access the Website

Residents can open Ready Preparedness using a cellphone, tablet, or computer.

  1. Open your internet browser.
  2. Visit the Ready Preparedness website link.
  3. Explore the available sections using the navigation menu.

2. Install on Your Device

You may add Ready Preparedness to your device's Home Screen for easier access.

  • Android: Open the browser menu and look for "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen."
  • iPhone: Open the website in Safari, tap Share, then select "Add to Home Screen."

The available option may depend on your device and browser.

3. View Announcements

Stay updated with important information posted by the barangay.

  1. Open the Announcements section.
  2. Read the latest published announcements.
  3. Check the details and follow the instructions provided.

4. Read the Preparedness Guide

Learn important safety instructions for different emergency situations.

  1. Open Preparedness Guide.
  2. Select or read the available topics.
  3. Follow the instructions to help you prepare.

5. Use the Go Bag Checklist

Check the emergency essentials that you already have prepared.

  1. Open Go Bag Checklist.
  2. Review the listed emergency items.
  3. Tick the checkbox for items you already have.

6. Find Evacuation Centers

View the designated evacuation centers in your barangay.

  1. Open Evacuation Centers.
  2. Read the names of the listed locations.
  3. Follow official barangay instructions during evacuation.

7. Check Emergency Hotlines

Find important contact numbers that may help during emergencies.

  1. Open Emergency Hotlines.
  2. Find the contact number you need.
  3. Use the number only for its intended emergency purpose.

8. Check the First Aid Kit

Review the basic supplies that should be available in your first aid kit.

  1. Open First Aid Kit.
  2. Review the listed supplies.
  3. Tick the items that you already have prepared.

9. Read Weather Reminders

Check weather-related reminders and safety information published on the website.

  1. Open Weather Reminders.
  2. Read the available reminders.
  3. Follow official weather advisories and warnings.

10. Check Notifications

Use the notification bell to view notifications available on the website.

  1. Look for the bell icon on the header.
  2. Tap or click the bell.
  3. Read the notifications displayed in the panel.

Push notifications may require device permission and additional setup before they become available.

Important Reminder

Ready Preparedness is an information resource for residents. During an emergency, always follow official announcements and instructions from barangay officials and authorized emergency responders.
